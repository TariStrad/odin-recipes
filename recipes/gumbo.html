Gumbo

Home

Description

This Authentic Louisiana Gumbo is made with a dark roux, vegetables, chicken, sausage, and shrimp, and served over rice. This is a beloved recipe shared with me by my Creole grandmother.

Ingredients

  • All Purpose FLour
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Celery
  • Bell Pepper
  • Yellow Onion
  • Green Onions
  • Freshly Chopped Parsley
  • Garlic
  • Cajun Seasoning
  • Chicken Broth
  • Andouille Sausages
  • Rotisserie Chicken
  • Shrimp
  • Cooked Rice

Steps

  1. Make the Roux
  2. Brown the sausage
  3. Cook the vegetables in broth
  4. Add broth, veggies, parsley, garlic, and roux to pot and stir well
  5. Add Meat
  6. Taste and serve
